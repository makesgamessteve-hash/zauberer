## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Burns, tears, wet and dry
The carpet SHALL burn low over flames, tear skidding along the ground fast, soak in rain and on water when out in the open, and dry with time, warmth and a fire; wet or badly torn it SHALL fly slower and climb less, and at zero integrity it SHALL not fly.

#### Scenario: Burns, tears, wet and dry
- **WHEN** a carpet is low and high over flames, skids fast, is rained on out in the open and worn, dries with and without a fire, rests on water, and burns to nothing
- **THEN** it burns only low, tears, soaks only in the open, flies slower and climbs less when soaked, dries faster by a fire, soaks on water, and torn to the weft it cannot fly and says so

### Requirement: Patching and upgrades
A field patch SHALL take 2 thread and 2 life or void mana and mend 20 up to 70, making a grounded carpet airworthy again from 10; the workshop SHALL repair fully; upgrades SHALL be woven into 3 slots and give their effects.

#### Scenario: Patching and upgrades
- **WHEN** a grounded carpet is patched without thread, without mana, with fibres and void mana, again with hides and life mana, then repaired fully, and upgrades are woven in, twice, unknown and past the slots
- **THEN** patching is refused for each want, mends to airworthy and up to 70 only, the repair makes it whole, weatherproof keeps rain off, the ward lining lets a third of a burn through, the swift weave makes it faster, and the slots and condition are kept with the game

### Requirement: Condition in the game
The game SHALL tick the carpet's condition from the weather, flames under it, the ground and fires near; flight SHALL follow its limits; on foot by the carpet M SHALL patch it; the HUD SHALL show it and the save keep it.

#### Scenario: Condition in the game
- **WHEN** it rains on the carpet for hours, it flies at full throttle, burns low over a fire, is torn to the weft and told to climb, lands, and is patched on foot
- **THEN** it soaks and the HUD shows it, it is slower, the flames burn it, it sinks though told to climb, the patch mends it airworthy and the HUD says so, and the save keeps it
