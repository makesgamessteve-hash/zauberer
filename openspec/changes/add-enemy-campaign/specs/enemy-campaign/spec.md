## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Azhdar's campaign
The campaign SHALL target settlements within 1,500 km of Azhdar's origin and all five Circle sites, strike only when due (sooner the more beats are done), burn a place (destroyed by the Ashen, its region blighted, refugees on the road to the nearest place standing) and weaken the ley.

#### Scenario: Azhdar's campaign
- **WHEN** the campaign is planned and struck at and before its time, slow and fast
- **THEN** it waits, burns, blights, sends refugees, weakens the ley, and strikes sooner with progress
