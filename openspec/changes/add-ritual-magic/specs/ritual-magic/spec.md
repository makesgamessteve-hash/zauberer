## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Laying the circle
A ritual SHALL be begun only on foot, at its time, with its ingredients, one at a time; channelled, it SHALL draw its mana from the land and then the mage, and when done act for its hours; leaving the circle, being hurt or lacking mana SHALL break it with backlash by what was drawn; mastery SHALL make it cheaper; effects SHALL be saved.

#### Scenario: Laying the circle
- **WHEN** Call rain is begun in the air, at noon, without water, then properly and again, channelled to the end, then broken in three ways, cast by a master, and its effects saved
- **THEN** each wrong beginning is refused with the reason, the mana comes from the mage at half and at the end, the rain is held for its hours and then ends, each breaking lashes back, the master pays half, and the effects come back

### Requirement: Rituals in the game
/ SHALL lay the circle for the chosen ritual and ' choose the next; Call rain SHALL bring rain over the land for its hours, Ward the camp keep beasts out and Unfold a room open a room of the pocket home.

#### Scenario: Rituals in the game
- **WHEN** in a drought the mage calls rain at noon and at dusk, walks off a second ritual, wards the camp against a wolf, and at night by the tent unfolds a room
- **THEN** noon is refused, at dusk the rain falls and ends after six hours, walking off breaks it and hurts, the wolf keeps out, and a new room unfolds
