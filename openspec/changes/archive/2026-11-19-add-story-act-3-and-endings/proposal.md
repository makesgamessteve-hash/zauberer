# Proposal: Act III and the endings

## Why

Act III leads to Azhdar's drifting citadel and a duel; then the heirs choose an ending
(concept 1.6).

## What Changes

- `story/finale.lua`: the citadel circling the nexus, divination (ritual, three Weaves or deep
  mastery), readiness (ritual mastery, ash ward, summoned ally), the duel, the endings open.
- Data: the ash ward enchantment, the divination and calm-the-storm rituals.
- In the game: the duel takes the heirs' real casts; F5 shows the endings, use takes one.
- M10. No platform differences: Lua on the engine's world, the same on desktop, web and WebXR.

## Impact

- New capability `story-act-3-and-endings`. Co-op: the heirs fight the duel together; the host chooses.
