## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The ending shown
After an ending is chosen the game SHALL show its name and closing lines and the chronicle of the heirs' own people, then the credits (skippable), then return to play in the world after the ending; the world SHALL wait while it is shown alone, and the save SHALL record that it was seen.

#### Scenario: After Bind
- **WHEN** each ending is chosen, and after Bind the credits are opened and skipped
- **THEN** each ending's name and its people's chronicle are shown, the save records it, the credits follow Bind and are skipped, and play goes on in the same world
