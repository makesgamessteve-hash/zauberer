## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Mages and how they take the heirs
Mad mages and Azhdar's servants SHALL be hostile, territorial ones hostile unless the heirs are stronger, greedy ones trade, others teach; truces, apprenticeships and alliances SHALL be taken by nature; a greedy ally SHALL betray once Azhdar holds three Weaves; contests are duels against the mage's colours.

#### Scenario: Mages and how they take the heirs
- **WHEN** every nature is met and offered every relation
- **THEN** each answers as described, the greedy ally betrays, and contests have their weaknesses

### Requirement: A domain of the heirs' own
A mage defeated SHALL leave the heirs their domain if they claim it: five mana a day for each region and the servants.

#### Scenario: A domain of the heirs' own
- **WHEN** a mage is defeated with and without a claim
- **THEN** the claimed domain gives its mana and servants, the unclaimed nothing

### Requirement: Rival mages in the world
The world SHALL hold mages at towers and elder ruins and the three lieutenants, complete; over days their domains grow and they trade or raid.

#### Scenario: Rival mages in the world
- **WHEN** the mages are made and sixty days pass
- **THEN** the lieutenants are there, domains have grown and there was trade or raiding
