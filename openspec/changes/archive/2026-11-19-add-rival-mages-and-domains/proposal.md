# Proposal: Rival mages and domains

## Why

Rival mages are the hardest regular fights and the most varied relations (concept 7.6).

## What Changes

- `story/rivals.lua`: mages from mage towers and elder ruins and Azhdar's three lieutenants, with
  personality, colours, discipline, sanctum, domain, servants and familiar; days: claims, raids,
  trade; meeting, truce, apprenticeship, alliance, betrayal; contests as duels; the heirs' own domain.
- `story/duel.lua`: the duel model shared with Azhdar.
- M10. No platform differences: Lua on the engine's world, the same on desktop, web and WebXR.

## Impact

- New capability `rival-mages-and-domains`. Co-op: the host runs the mages; domains are the family's.
