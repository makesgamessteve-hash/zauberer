# Proposal: The heirs

## Why

All players are Farrokh's children (concept 11.1, 11.3): one to four heirs, each made by its
player, present or resting in the pocket home, the latecomers met again at a reunion.

## What Changes

- `coop/heirs.lua`: the count set by the host, character creation (girl or boy, name, age 9-14,
  twins), joining (a reunion owed when the prologue is past), leaving (resting, studying or tending
  the garden), the shared and the personal, which fragments of the hiding each heir sees.
- `coop/play.lua`: the host keeps the heirs; players send their heir as they made it.
- Platform: none. M9.

## Impact

- New capability `heirs`. Saved with the game; co-op: the host's record is the truth.
