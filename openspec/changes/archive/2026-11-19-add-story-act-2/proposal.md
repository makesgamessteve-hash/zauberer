# Proposal: Act II: the Circle's remnants and the Great Weaves

## Why

Act II is a sandbox with story threads (concept 1.5): the Circle's remnants teach, reveal the
past and hold Great Weaves the heirs can secure before Azhdar.

## What Changes

- Remnants (teacher, student, grave, tower) teach their discipline, tell the next revelation and
  give up their Weave - or tell that Azhdar came first; the Circle's legacy and the revelations
  are beats.
- M10. No platform differences: Lua on the engine's world, the same on desktop, web and WebXR.

## Impact

- New capability `story-act-2`. Co-op: Weaves secured are the family's.
