## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The Circle's remnants
Meeting a remnant once SHALL teach its discipline (teachers and students), tell the next revelation into the journal and secure its Weave, or tell that Azhdar took it first; meetings are saved.

#### Scenario: The Circle's remnants
- **WHEN** the hermit is met twice and the astronomer after Azhdar
- **THEN** summoning is taught and the Deep Weave secured once, and the astronomer's Weave is gone

### Requirement: The Great Weaves
Azhdar's campaign SHALL go for every Circle site: he takes the Weaves the heirs have not secured and is foiled where they have.

#### Scenario: The Great Weaves
- **WHEN** the campaign runs with one Weave secured
- **THEN** four are taken and one foiled
