## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The prologue's way
The story's HUD SHALL name the next place of the prologue (the chores' places, Farrokh's house, the hatch, the ruins, the rings), its distance and compass direction from the heir; the village's fires SHALL be out on the morning after it burns and its doors burnt (a door opened or shut before stays gone in the ruin); the carpet that chose the heir SHALL be unrolled beside them once they are above the ground, and saved so.

#### Scenario: The prologue's way
- **WHEN** the prologue is played through
- **THEN** the HUD names the qanat outlet, then the goat pen, the rings and the way out of the valley; the cellar keeps the fire's heat out; there is no fire by morning and no door left in Farrokh's house; and up from the cellar the carpet is beside the heir and takes them
