# Proposal: The world after the ending

## Why

After any ending the world continues with consequences (concept 1.6).

## What Changes

- `story/aftermath.lua`: the ley restored (Reweave), raised (Succession) or fading by 0.08 a year to
  0.1 (Unravel); realms and faiths take the heirs as saviours or tyrants, faiths that abhor magic
  distrust the Reweave; the Ashen broken; each people's chronicle of the end.
- M10. No platform differences: Lua on the engine's world, the same on desktop, web and WebXR.

## Impact

- New capability `post-game-world-state`. Co-op: the host's world after the ending is everyone's.
