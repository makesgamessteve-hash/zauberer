## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Each people's chronicle of the end
Each people SHALL tell the end by its values and its view of magic (witch-children where magic is feared or forbidden; kneeling or cursing tyrants); after Unravel the ley SHALL fade each year to no less than 0.1, after Bind stay.

#### Scenario: Each people's chronicle of the end
- **WHEN** the end is told by two peoples and years pass after Unravel and Bind
- **THEN** the chronicles differ as their peoples, the ley fades to its floor after Unravel and stays after Bind

### Requirement: The world after the ending
After an ending realms SHALL regard the heirs as saviours (tyrants after Succession), the ley SHALL be whole after Reweave and fade after Unravel, and a chronicle SHALL be written.

#### Scenario: The world after the ending
- **WHEN** each ending is taken
- **THEN** the regard, the ley and the chronicle follow it
