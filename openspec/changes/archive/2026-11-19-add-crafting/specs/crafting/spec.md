## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The workbench
A craft SHALL name its goods (thread from any fibre counting by its worth) and its hours; crafting SHALL tell what is lacking, take the goods and give the thing; the carpet's repair SHALL make it whole, and be refused when it is whole.

#### Scenario: The workbench
- **WHEN** a cord is made without enough fibre and with it, a horn lantern made, and the torn carpet mended without cord, with it, and again whole
- **THEN** the lacking thread is told, the cord takes half an hour and leaves the spare fibre, the lantern takes its goods, the repair wants cord and then mends the carpet fully, and a whole carpet is not mended
