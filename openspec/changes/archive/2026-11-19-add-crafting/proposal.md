# Proposal: Crafting at the workbench

## Why

The M7 exit demo's lantern is made before it is enchanted; the workshop's bench turns gathered
goods into things (concept 5.12) and mends the carpet fully.

## What Changes

- **Crafts** (`data/crafts.lua`, `magic/crafting.lua`): cord twisted from any fibres (thread), a
  horn lantern (deadwood, resin, hide, cord), a staff, a wand, and mending the carpet whole.
- What is lacking is told; the hours of the work pass.

## Impact

- New capability `crafting`. New items: cord, horn lantern, staff, wand.
