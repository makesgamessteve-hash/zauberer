## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: The menu
The menu SHALL be the father's book: tabbed pages (Menu, Journal, Map, Help, Settings), the Menu page offering resume, save and load, company, controls, packages, credits and quit; up and down choose, left and right change a setting, confirm opens or does it, Q and E turn the tabs, Escape goes back and closes the book; every row and tab SHALL also be reachable with the mouse and by touch.

#### Scenario: The menu
- **WHEN** the book is used to change the language on the Settings page, look at packages and credits, resume and quit
- **THEN** each does what it says
