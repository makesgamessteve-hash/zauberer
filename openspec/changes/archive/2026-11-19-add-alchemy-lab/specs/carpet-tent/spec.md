## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Rooms of the pocket home
The pocket home SHALL have rooms from data in a walking order, the hearth room, bedchamber, study, vault, alchemy laboratory and workshop open from the start and others opened later; each SHALL offer what it is for; inside it SHALL be 20 C, sheltered and still, the hearth burning.

#### Scenario: Rooms of the pocket home
- **WHEN** a new home is walked through both ways, its warmth felt in the bedchamber and by the hearth, the garden opened twice and a ballroom asked for, and the home is saved and read back, also from broken data
- **THEN** the open rooms are walked through and the closed ones passed by, the bedchamber offers sleep and the hearth cooking, inside is warm, sheltered and still with a fire at the hearth, the garden opens once and is told, the ballroom does not exist, and the home comes back
