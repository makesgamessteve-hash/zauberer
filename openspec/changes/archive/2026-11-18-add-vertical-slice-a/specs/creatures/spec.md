## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Harm and death
Creatures SHALL be hurt by flames and heat, thick smoke and fast bodies, and by the scripts with a kind of harm, less their resistance to it; healing SHALL not exceed full health; pain SHALL frighten them; at no health a creature SHALL die, a dead flier SHALL fall to the ground, and the death SHALL be told with its cause, and for a magical creature its colour and motes.

#### Scenario: Harm and death
- **WHEN** a deer is hurt and healed, a storm bird resisting smoke breathes it and is struck down, a deer stands in a fire, and a boulder falls on a wolf
- **THEN** health drops and returns to full, pain frightens, the bird takes half the smoke, its death is told with the cause and it is magical, the dead feel nothing more and the bird falls to the ground, the fire burns the deer and the boulder hurts the wolf
