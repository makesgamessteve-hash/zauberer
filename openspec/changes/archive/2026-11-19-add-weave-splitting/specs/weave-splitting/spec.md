## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Riding together
Riding the whole Weave, one heir SHALL pilot (another taking over when the pilot leaves) and the others ride and cast; the whole has its full lift, speed, ceiling and mana.

#### Scenario: Riding together
- **WHEN** three heirs ride and the pilot changes
- **THEN** one pilots, the others cast, and the figures are whole

### Requirement: Splitting and rejoining
The Weave SHALL split into a piece per heir (two at least), each weaker by its share and bond with its own cloak, taking a moment; rejoin only with the heirs within 20 m, the pieces' damage becoming the whole's; the tent SHALL need the whole Weave unless Homing is woven in.

#### Scenario: Splitting and rejoining
- **WHEN** the Weave splits, is hurt, rejoins far and near, and Homing is woven
- **THEN** the pieces are weaker, the far rejoin is refused, the near one adds the damage, and with Homing a piece may pitch the tent
