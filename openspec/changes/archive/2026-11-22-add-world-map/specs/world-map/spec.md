## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The map
The map SHALL show the land the heir has seen (within 2 km of where they went, by height as sea, lowland, highland or peak; unseen land blank), the places they came near by name, the heir, the carpet when on foot and the current goal, measured from where the carpet set out; it SHALL open on the heir, move with the stick, zoom out through 1 to 20 km a letter, and be kept with the save.

#### Scenario: Seen and unseen
- **WHEN** the heir flies 20 km east, a place is come near twice, the map is opened from the menu, moved and zoomed, and saved and restored
- **THEN** the flown strip is drawn and land 10 km north is blank, the heir sits 10 km east of the middle, the place is named once, the goal and the carpet are marked, the map opens on the heir, moves east and zooms out, and comes back from the save
