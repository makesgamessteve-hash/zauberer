## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Votes on time
Sleep and time skips SHALL pass when every present heir is in the pocket home or has agreed, be refused when one asks to wait, expire after 30 s; the host's clock SHALL be followed by every client.

#### Scenario: Votes on time
- **WHEN** votes are cast every way
- **THEN** they pass, are refused or expire as described

### Requirement: Rituals, scaling, friendly fire and revival
A ritual of n heirs SHALL take its time over 1 + 0.5(n - 1) and each give a share of the mana; groups, boldness and mages SHALL grow with heirs, resources not; spells SHALL harm heirs fully unless softened (a quarter); a downed heir SHALL be revivable by another with life magic or medicine for 60 s, then carried home.

#### Scenario: Rituals, scaling, friendly fire and revival
- **WHEN** rituals, scaling, harm and revival are asked
- **THEN** each is as described
