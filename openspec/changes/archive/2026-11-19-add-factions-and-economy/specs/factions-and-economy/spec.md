## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Prices by supply and demand
A good's price SHALL follow supply within reach and demand by population, dearer to buy than to sell.

#### Scenario: Prices by supply and demand
- **WHEN** salt and copper are priced on a coast and in the hills
- **THEN** salt is cheaper on the coast and copper in the hills, buying dearer than selling

### Requirement: Coins in towns, barter in villages
Towns SHALL take coin; villages SHALL barter goods of the same worth; trading SHALL raise standing a little.

#### Scenario: Coins in towns, barter in villages
- **WHEN** the heir buys in a city and a village and sells in a village
- **THEN** the city wants coin, the village takes dates, sells for bread, and remembers it kindly

### Requirement: Factions and standing
Factions SHALL have a stance on mages; deeds (saving from fire, clearing bandits, theft, broken guest right, magic cast) SHALL move standing in the place, its realm and faith, and prosperity; standing is saved.

#### Scenario: Factions and standing
- **WHEN** deeds are done in Anarvan
- **THEN** standing and prosperity move as described and come back from the save

### Requirement: Caravans on the trade routes
Caravans SHALL travel each trade route back and forth at 35 km a day along the great circle, reaching its ends.

#### Scenario: Caravans on the trade routes
- **WHEN** a caravan is followed six hours and to its road's end
- **THEN** it moves at a caravan's pace and arrives
