## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: The map
The map SHALL be painted on the book's pages from the land the heir has seen (within 2 km of where they went, by height as sea, shore, lowland, highland or peak in the parchment's inks; unseen land left as paper), with the way flown, the places they came near by name, the heir, the carpet when on foot, the other heirs in company and the current goal, a compass rose and a scale, measured from where the carpet set out; it SHALL open on the heir, move with the stick, arrows, mouse drag or a finger, zoom with the triggers, the wheel or a pinch from 1 to 20 km a step of the scale, and be kept with the save.

#### Scenario: Seen and unseen
- **WHEN** the heir flies 20 km east, a place is come near twice, the map is opened from the book, moved and zoomed, and saved and restored
- **THEN** the flown strip is painted and land 10 km north is paper, the heir sits 10 km east of the middle, the place is named once, the goal and the carpet are marked, the map opens on the heir, moves east and zooms out, and comes back from the save
