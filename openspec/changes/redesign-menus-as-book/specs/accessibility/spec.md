## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: The menu
The menu SHALL be the father's book: tabbed pages (Journal, Map, Bag, Weaves, Codex, Settings) and a corner menu (Save, Load, Company in a session, Help, Credits, Quit to title); up and down choose, left and right change a setting, confirm opens or does it, Q and E turn the tabs, Escape goes back and closes the book; every entry SHALL also be reachable with the mouse and by touch.

#### Scenario: The menu
- **WHEN** the book is used to change the language on the Settings page, look at packages and credits from the corner menu, resume and quit
- **THEN** each does what it says
