## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Practice, study and trials
Each discipline SHALL rise through five tiers by experience from practice and study, held at each tier's threshold until its trial is passed; each tier SHALL improve its work; tiers gained SHALL be told; a page SHALL show them; mastery SHALL be saved, broken saves keeping what they can.

#### Scenario: Practice, study and trials
- **WHEN** one kind of brew is practised a lot, then another; ritual is studied and then practised; the page and HUD are read; mastery is saved and broken saves read
- **THEN** alchemy is held at the threshold until two kinds are brewed, then Apprentice and told, its factor better; study holds at the threshold until the trial; the page and HUD show tiers and the trial waiting; mastery comes back

### Requirement: The M7 exit demo
The game SHALL let the mage gather healing herbs and brew a healing draught, make and enchant a lantern of perpetual light, call rain over a drought at dusk, summon a water elemental from a lake and bond a familiar, every discipline growing.

#### Scenario: The M7 exit demo
- **WHEN** the mage flies to the spring meadows, gathers herbs, brews in the laboratory, makes and enchants a lantern in the workshop, calls rain at dusk in a drought, summons by the nearest deep water and bonds a fox
- **THEN** the draughts are brewed, the lantern burns on the land's light through the night, rain falls and the ground soaks it up, the elemental forms, the fox is a familiar, and alchemy, artificing, ritual and summoning have grown
