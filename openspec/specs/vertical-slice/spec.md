# vertical-slice Specification

## Purpose
Defines the vertical slices the milestones end with: short playable demos in which
the systems of a milestone meet, played through as scenarios.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Vertical Slice A
The base game SHALL let a player, in one session near the start: scare a wolf pack away with fire, see the grass catch and put it out with Unmake Fire, bring down the storm bird with a Fireball and collect its motes, and at deep water (a lake, or the sea in the first world) step off, be stopped by water too deep to wade, freeze it and walk out onto the ice, then fly on, alive.

#### Scenario: Vertical Slice A
- **WHEN** the slice is played through with the hands, the spellbook and the bindings of the base game
- **THEN** the wolves run from the fire, the fire spreads and is put out, the storm bird falls and its storm mana is collected, the deep water stops the mage, the frozen water carries the mage out from the shore, and the carpet flies on with the player alive

### Requirement: Vertical Slice B
Begun with F5, the prologue in Anarvan (set piece: Farrokh's house with the hidden sanctum, qanat, pen, orchards) SHALL run through chores, Azhdar's arrival, the handover, hiding while the village burns, the ashes and burial, the sanctum, the carpet bonding (it does not fly before), the first flight, survival and leaving the valley.

#### Scenario: Vertical Slice B
- **WHEN** the slice is played through
- **THEN** every beat follows from what the heir does, the village is laid in ashes, the Primer's weaves are learnt, and the carpet flies only once bonded

### Requirement: The prologue's way
The story's HUD SHALL name the next place of the prologue (the chores' places, Farrokh's house, the hatch, the ruins, the rings), its distance and compass direction from the heir; the village's fires SHALL be out on the morning after it burns and its doors burnt (a door opened or shut before stays gone in the ruin); the carpet that chose the heir SHALL be unrolled beside them once they are above the ground, and saved so.

#### Scenario: The prologue's way
- **WHEN** the prologue is played through
- **THEN** the HUD names the qanat outlet, then the goat pen, the rings and the way out of the valley; the cellar keeps the fire's heat out; there is no fire by morning and no door left in Farrokh's house; and up from the cellar the carpet is beside the heir and takes them
