# summoning Specification

## Purpose
Summoning beings formed from local matter and kept by mana, and bonding wild animals as familiars that follow the mage.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Bound beings
A creature bound to the player SHALL follow within its distance, fear and hunt the player no more, and let go SHALL be itself again; binding no creature SHALL fail.

#### Scenario: Bound beings
- **WHEN** a deer far off and a hungry wolf are bound to the mage, and the deer let go
- **THEN** binding nothing fails, the deer follows to the mage unafraid, the wolf does not hunt the mage, and the freed deer flees

### Requirement: Summoning in the game
"," SHALL summon a water elemental when water is within 20 m, else a wisp, bound and following; upkeep SHALL be paid from the mage's mana and unpaid the binding weaken until the being fades; "-" SHALL dismiss; ";" SHALL bond a nearby animal offered its food and life mana as a familiar, kept with the game.

#### Scenario: Summoning in the game
- **WHEN** the mage summons away from water and dismisses, summons by a pool and walks, runs out of water mana, then bonds a fox without and with meat, and the game is saved
- **THEN** a wisp comes and is dismissed, an elemental forms from the pool and follows, unpaid it fades, the fox wants meat, then is the mage's familiar, keeps close, and is kept
