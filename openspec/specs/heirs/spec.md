# heirs Specification

## Purpose
The heirs: one to four of Farrokh's children made by their players, present or resting in the pocket home, met again at a reunion.

## Requirements

### Requirement: One to four heirs
A world SHALL have one to four heirs as the host set; each SHALL be made as a girl or boy with a name of 1 to 20 letters and an age of 9 to 14, twins sharing an age; heirs SHALL be saved and age with time.

#### Scenario: One to four heirs
- **WHEN** heirs are made with good and bad choices and saved
- **THEN** the good ones are made, each bad one is refused with why, and they come back from the save

### Requirement: Present and absent heirs
A joining heir SHALL be present (a reunion owed if they missed the prologue); a leaving one SHALL rest in the pocket home; the shared state and each heir's own SHALL be kept apart; each heir SHALL see other fragments of the hiding.

#### Scenario: Present and absent heirs
- **WHEN** heirs join before and after the prologue, leave, and the state is split
- **THEN** the latecomer is owed a reunion, the leaver rests, shared and personal are apart, and the fragments differ
