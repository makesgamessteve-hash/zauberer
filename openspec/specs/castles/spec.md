# castles Specification

## Purpose
Citadels and castles: sited on high ground, curtain walls along the defensible contour, gatehouses, baileys and palaces, their states and styles.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Siting and the curtain wall
A castle SHALL stand on the highest ground within 300 m, its curtain wall of 16 segments following where the ground falls away, with wall-walks and crenels, towers, a gatehouse with portcullis and murder holes, the palace and the outer buildings, cellars, a dungeon and a secret way out; 50 to 160 rooms.

#### Scenario: Siting and the curtain wall
- **WHEN** a castle is planned near Anarvan
- **THEN** it is on the high ground with its walls, towers, gate, buildings, cellars and passage and a fitting number of rooms

### Requirement: States and styles
A castle SHALL be held, abandoned, ruined, held by bandits, a rival mage or the Ashen (or as asked), its garrison by its state; each family's style gives its wall material, towers and height.

#### Scenario: States and styles
- **WHEN** forty castles are planned and one asked to be the Ashen's
- **THEN** held, abandoned and ruined ones are among them with fitting garrisons, the Ashen's is theirs, and every style is complete
