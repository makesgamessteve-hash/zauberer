# 7. Gameplay

## 7.1 Core loops

```
 Moment to moment (seconds)       Session (30–90 min)             Long term (hours to weeks)
 ─────────────────────────        ───────────────────             ──────────────────────────
 fly · look · sense               expedition out from the tent    knowledge (Codex, weaves)
 gather · hunt · forage           ↓                               mastery of six disciplines
 cast · dodge · fight             discover · gather · survive     mana capacity and attunement
 collect motes                    ↓                               carpet, bag, tent upgrades
 react to fire, weather, beasts   return · craft · brew · enchant domains and Great Weaves
                                  research · plan · sleep         the hunt for Azhdar
```

1. **Explore.** Fly over an endless planet that is worth looking at. Discovery itself is
   rewarded with Codex entries, landmarks, ruins, nexuses and story rumours.
2. **Gather.** Plants, minerals, creature parts, mana motes and crystals. Knowing *how*
   and *when* to harvest matters.
3. **Survive.** Food, water, warmth and rest, which matter most in the early game.
4. **Learn.** Experiment, study and find teachers. Knowledge is the main currency.
5. **Craft.** Alchemy, artificing and cooking turn gathered materials into power.
6. **Master.** Higher discipline tiers unlock bigger magic: rituals that reshape the land,
   summons and Great Weaves.
7. **Pursue.** Story threads lead toward Azhdar, and the world darkens if ignored.

## 7.2 Survival

| Need | Driven by | Managed by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Hunger** | Calories burned (exertion, cold) | Foraging, hunting, fishing, trading, cooking (cooked food gives more nutrition and is safer), preservation |
| **Thirst** | Heat, exertion, salty food | Rivers, rain, wells; boiling or purifying swamp water; *Create Water* |
| **Body temperature** | Air temperature, wind chill, wetness, altitude, insulation, shelter | Cloak, clothing, fire, tent, warm food and drink, potions, wards |
| **Fatigue** | Time awake, exertion, mana strain | Sleep (tent, inns, rough camp with risks) |
| **Health** | Injuries, burns, frostbite, falls, poison, disease | Rest, herbal medicine, alchemy, Life magic |
| **Conditions** | Poisoned, sick, infected wound, intoxicated, soaked, smoke-choked | Specific cures from the Codex and alchemy |

- **Difficulty presets:** *Wanderer* (needs drain slowly, no disease), *Survivor*
  (default), *Hermit* (harsh: full disease, spoilage and exposure).
- **The child's limits:** a low carry weight outside the bag, little melee strength, and
  quickly tired. Magic, cleverness and the carpet compensate. With optional aging the
  protagonist gains some physical strength over time.

## 7.3 Knowledge and the Codex

- The **journal** collects story events, rumours, maps and notes. The player can also
  write their own.
- The **Codex** is a natural-history encyclopaedia:
  - species of plants, fungi, animals and insects,
  - minerals and substances,
  - phenomena (weather, ley lines, strata and fossils),
  - peoples, languages, faiths, realms, places and history (see
    [World history §10.11](10-world-history.md#1011-the-history-database-and-lore)).
    History entries collect each culture's version of an event side by side, so the
    player can compare accounts and piece together what really happened.
- **Entry levels:** *Seen → Observed → Studied → Understood*. Each level reveals more
  properties: what it is, where it lives, what it is good for, how to harvest it and what
  is dangerous about it.
- **Sources:** observation (look at a thing for a few seconds; longer for creatures),
  sampling, lab analysis and dissection, books and teachers. Tasting an unknown plant is
  possible, and risky.
- The **spellbook** holds known weaves, ritual diagrams, alchemical recipes and artifact
  patterns. It is a diegetic book with pages and the player's own annotations.

## 7.4 Progression

There are no character levels. Power comes from:

| Axis | Grows by |
| --- | --- |
| Discipline mastery (6) | Practice, study and trials (see [Magic](03-magic.md)) |
| Weave familiarity | Using each weave |
| Mana reservoir | Repeated deep drains and recovery, rituals at nexuses, rare artifacts |
| Channeling rate and control | Spellcasting practice, focus artifacts |
| Color attunement (8) | Using a color, living in places rich in it, food and potions |
| Codex knowledge | Observation and study |
| Equipment | Artifacts, clothing, carpet, bag and tent upgrades |
| Allies | Familiars, summons, friendly NPCs, domains |
| Great Weaves | Story artifacts with unique powers |

## 7.5 Combat

Combat is a test of everything else. The player rarely wins by raw damage alone.

- **Aerial magic combat:** strafing runs on the carpet, the left and right hand weaves,
  and charge and release.
- **Environmental tactics:**
  - set grass on fire upwind of enemies, or freeze the lake they stand on,
  - collapse a cliff, or flood the valley,
  - blind them with light at night, or hide in smoke.
- **Ground combat** is weaker, because the child is fragile. Stealth (cloak camouflage,
  scent and wind) and avoidance are valid choices.
- **Enemies:**
  - predators and territorial beasts,
  - magical creatures,
  - bandits,
  - rival mages: the hardest regular fights, since they use the same weave system as the
    player,
  - the Ashen and Azhdar's constructs (ash golems, cinder hounds),
  - Azhdar's lieutenants (boss duels).
- **Damage model:** damage is mostly *physical through the simulation*. Heat burns,
  impact bruises and breaks, cold freezes, poison is a substance in the body, and smoke
  suffocates. Wards and resistances act on these channels.
- **Mana motes** drop from defeated magical beings. Collecting them mid-fight is part of
  the rhythm, and it is an homage to the genre roots.

## 7.6 Rival mages

- **Procedurally generated mages** each have:
  - a personality (greedy, scholarly, territorial, kind, mad),
  - favoured colors and disciplines,
  - a sanctum and domain, servants, a familiar and a reputation.
- **They live in the global simulation.** They expand domains, raid each other, trade with
  settlements and react to the player's growing power.
- **Possible relationships:** rivalry, a truce or trade agreement, an apprenticeship
  (teaching), an alliance against Azhdar, or betrayal.
- **Azhdar's lieutenants** are named rival mages with authored traits and story roles.

## 7.7 Death, saving and time

- **Defeat is not game over.** When health reaches zero, the carpet carries the
  unconscious child away to the last tent site or a safe spot nearby. This costs time,
  some carried (non-bag) items, carpet integrity and a lingering weakness. On *Hermit*
  difficulty there is an optional permadeath mode.
- **Saving:** the game autosaves regularly and when the player sleeps. There is also a
  manual save from the bedchamber or the menu. Several save slots, and one planet per
  save.
- **Time:** day and night, seasons and years pass. Sleeping, travel and long rituals
  advance time. In-game time doesn't stop on its own, but the game can be paused.

## 7.8 Example first two hours (vertical slice target)

1. Prologue in Anarvan: chores, the attack, hiding.
2. Ashes. Bury Farrokh, find the sanctum, take the books and the bag.
3. First weaves: *Kindle*, *Light*, *Lift*. The carpet bonding dream. The first flight.
4. Forage berries and mushrooms (one is poisonous, and the Codex warns at *Observed*).
   Boil water. Spend the first night in the tent: hearth room and bedchamber.
5. Follow the scorched trail. A wolf pack at dusk: scare them off with fire, but the dry
   grass catches. Learn *Unmake Fire* in a panic, or flee upwind.
6. Reach a farmstead whose survivors point to "the old woman in the marsh who knows
   herbs". This is the first teacher, and alchemy unlocks.
