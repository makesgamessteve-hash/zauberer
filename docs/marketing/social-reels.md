# Social media reels

Short videos of Zauberer for TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube, cut from
real in-game footage. Nothing is mocked up: the shots are rendered by the
engine from `tools/reels/reel.lua`, and the music and ambience are the game's
own (generated by `tools/gen/gen_sounds.py`, CC BY 4.0).

## The videos

| File | Size | Length | Use it for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `reel-9x16.mp4` | 1080×1920, 30 fps | ~35 s | TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts |
| `feed-4x5.mp4` | 1080×1350, 30 fps | ~35 s | Instagram feed and Facebook feed posts |
| `trailer-16x9.mp4` | 1920×1080, 30 fps | ~35 s | YouTube (regular video), Facebook video, X, the website |
| `cover-9x16.png` | 1080×1920 | | Cover frame for Reels, TikTok and Shorts |
| `cover-4x5.png` | 1080×1350 | | Cover for feed posts |
| `thumbnail-16x9.png` | 1280×720 | | YouTube thumbnail |

All are H.264 High (yuv420p) with AAC stereo at 48 kHz, `+faststart`, and
under every platform's size and length limit. In the portrait reel, captions
stay out of the areas the apps cover with their own buttons and text (the top
250 px, the bottom 450 px and the right 140 px).

The shots, in order:
1. A world growing: the creation's time-lapse globe.
2. The carpet, circled by the camera.
3. Low and fast out over a coast.
4. A village and its people.
5. Wolves stalking deer.
6. A grass fire running before the wind.
7. The fire burning on under the stars.
8. A storm, with rain and lightning.
9. Inside the tent, by the hearth.
10. The end card: the title, "Free in your browser" and the link.

## Making them again

```sh
cmake --build --preset release
# The footage (a window of each size; about 10 minutes each with llvmpipe)
SDL_VIDEO_DRIVER=offscreen ZB_WINDOW_SIZE=1080x1920 ZB_GOLDEN_OUT=out/portrait \
  build/release/bin/zauberer --assets assets --user-dir out/user-p \
  --scenario tools/reels/reel.lua > out/portrait.log
SDL_VIDEO_DRIVER=offscreen ZB_WINDOW_SIZE=1920x1080 ZB_GOLDEN_OUT=out/landscape \
  build/release/bin/zauberer --assets assets --user-dir out/user-l \
  --scenario tools/reels/reel.lua > out/landscape.log
# The videos and stills
python3 tools/reels/make_reels.py --portrait out/portrait --portrait-log out/portrait.log \
  --landscape out/landscape --landscape-log out/landscape.log --out out/reels
```

The captions, the music under each shot and the end card are at the top of
`tools/reels/make_reels.py`.
